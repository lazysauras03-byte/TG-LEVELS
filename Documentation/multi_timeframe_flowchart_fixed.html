STEP 3 — 3 MINUTE · ENTRY ZONE (9 EMA STRATEGY)

📈 IN AN UPTREND (BUY SETUP)

✔ Wait for price to pull back towards the 9 EMA band
✔ Once price touches or comes close to 9 EMA Low and shows signs of reversal → this becomes the entry zone
✔ A valid Higher Low or a Bullish Fractal forming near the EMA band confirms the entry

📉 IN A DOWNTREND (SELL SETUP)

✔ Wait for price to pull back towards the 9 EMA band
✔ Once price touches or comes close to 9 EMA High and shows signs of reversal → this becomes the entry zone
✔ A valid Higher High or a Bearish Fractal forming near the EMA band confirms the entry
🎯 The 3 minute entry zone acts as the final trigger — it is the point where all three timeframes align and the trade is executed with full confirmation from the top-down analysis.
